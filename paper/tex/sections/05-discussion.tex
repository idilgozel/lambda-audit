\section{Discussion and limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What the audit licenses.} Every prospective, passing result is
exact in-domain: the equilibrium map, the cost law and its threshold are
theorems about a linear-quadratic-Gaussian family, and the experiments confirm
that PPO with an observation-restricted critic lands where those theorems say.
Outside the family the paper has a proposition about \emph{absence} and one
positive external result. The bridge from the testbed to a benchmark is the
identification of the hidden coordinate's relaxation time with the horizon
that must exceed it; that is an analogy with a derivation behind it, not a
derivation, and E12 is what it looks like when the analogy is applied with the
wrong timescale. E14 registered the timescale from the system's dynamics and
bracketed the knee rather than pinning it; its step-down and above-knee
equivalence predictions passed and its variance-ordering prediction held on one
of two variants (\S\ref{sec:results}).

\paragraph{The live alternative.} A memoryless agent that does worse at low
$\lambda$ might simply be undertrained there. Two registered results
separate the accounts. The decoupled arm shares representation, optimiser,
budget and evaluator with the coupled arm and shows no $\lambda$ dependence
at all (P6.3, largest gain $0.0079$); undertraining would not respect the
scope proposition's boundary. And the open-loop control in the pilot (G2)
shows nothing where the proposition predicts nothing. What remains open is
whether the low-$\lambda$ \emph{memory}-arm variance (P9.3, a factor of 42.6)
is the mechanism's non-convergence or ordinary optimisation noise; the
demoted stability calculation predicts the former and the evidence is mixed.

\paragraph{Measurement.} Five seeds per cell and a 15M-step budget are the
resolution of every verdict here, and the equivalence tests state their
margins for that reason. Cluster nodes of different CPU generations produce
bit-different trajectories from identical seeds; the class of every job is
recorded, and the largest cross-class perturbation among seven reruns is
$1.9$ across-seed standard deviations, moving no verdict. One comparison
inherits a caveat: P7.2 orders instances whose cells were not all produced on
bitwise-comparable hardware. It failed as registered, so no claim rests on it.

\paragraph{Departures from the discipline.} A pre-submission audit found
two, and an audit paper does not get to hide its own findings. The scoring
code for P10.1--P10.3 was implemented after the data existed (constants and
predictions frozen 2026-08-14; the file's advertised scoring mode was a stub
until 2026-08-21, and the implemented scorer prints a sensitivity table over
the one aggregation choice the freeze had left open). The diagnostic
preregistration (E8) lacked a mechanical freeze assertion until the same date.
Two further post-data amendments are verdict-neutral by construction and
disclosed in the files that carry them: E12's scorer gained a duplicate-cell
refusal after its grid re-ran the probe's reference cells, and a crash guard
on its own success path. Git dates before 21 August are uninformative because
the initial commit was amended. Saved job logs corroborate run chronology
and result rows, but their timestamps are printed by the job script and do
not independently establish early freeze-before-data ordering. Later
separate pre-run commits provide additional ordering evidence
(Appendix~\ref{app:provenance}).

\paragraph{What we would do differently.} Capability probes before grids:
E11 spent 119 core-hours on a grid whose gates then failed, and every later
experiment runs two to four probe tasks first. Equivalence tests rather than
``CI contains zero'' for claims of no effect, which get harder to satisfy as
data improve; E14's equivalence criterion was itself revised before its freeze
when a power simulation showed the per-cell form failing its own registered
pattern on noise. Tolerances in absolute rather than relative units where the
target is small (E10's lesson). And a relaxation time, never an episode
length, to place a knee.

\paragraph{The artifact.} The preregistrations, scorers, the ledger
generator, the bitwise-verified environment ports, the harness and every
results row are released, with a chronology that says what the history can
and cannot show. A reader who disagrees with a threshold can rescore; a reader
who disagrees with the theory can regenerate every reference number. That,
rather than the count of passes, is what we think an audit is for.
