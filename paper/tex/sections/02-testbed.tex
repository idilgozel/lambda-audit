\section{An exactly solvable testbed for the bootstrap horizon}
\label{sec:testbed}

The audit needs a setting in which the equilibrium the learner is pulled
toward, the cost of sitting there, and the threshold at which learning stops
arriving are all computable exactly, so that a failed prediction indicts the
theory rather than the baseline. We use the smallest such setting that still
contains the phenomenon.

\subsection{Model and instances}

The state $s=(v,z)\in\mathbb{R}^2$ is a damped oscillator driven by noise,
\begin{equation}
\dot s = A s + B u + \xi, \qquad
A = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -c & -\gamma \end{pmatrix}, \quad
B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}, \quad
\mathbb{E}[\xi \xi^\top] = \mathrm{diag}(0,\, 2\Theta\gamma c),
\label{eq:model}
\end{equation}
discretised by exact zero-order hold at step $h$ ($F=e^{Ah}$, $G$ and
$\Sigma_d$ by quadrature to machine precision). The agent observes
\textbf{only} $v$; $z$ is hidden. Cost is $h(qv^2+ru^2)$, $q=r=1$, discounted
at $\beta=e^{-\rho h}$, $\rho=0.05$; $\Theta=1$, $c=0.3$. Exploration is a
pinned Gaussian on the action, $\sigma_e=0.3$, held fixed rather than learned
so that entropy collapse cannot act as an uncontrolled brake on the
$\lambda$ reading. The GLE form generalises to Prony kernels
$K(\tau)=\sum_i c_i e^{-\gamma_i\tau}$ (\S\ref{sec:theory}); the experiments
use the single-mode member.

Two timescales organise everything: the hidden coordinate decorrelates over
$\tau_z\approx 1/(\gamma h)$ steps, and GAE($\lambda$) bootstraps over
$(1-\beta\lambda)^{-1}$ steps. Their ratio is controlled through the single
product $\gamma h$, instantiated at $0.02, 0.05, 0.10, 0.20$
(instances g1\_h0.02, g1\_h0.05, g2\_h0.05, g4\_h0.05), so every predicted
threshold moves monotonically across the family and each prediction is tested
at four locations.

\subsection{Two arms: the effect's scope is part of the design}

``Memory helps'' and ``the critic's bias distorts learning'' are usually
observationally identical. They separate here because the critic's bias can
reach the policy through only one door: the expected advantage of action $a$
at $v$ depends on $\hat V$ only through $\mathbb{E}[\hat V(v_{t+1})\mid v_t,a]$
(\S\ref{sec:theory}, Proposition~1). Every in-domain experiment therefore runs
on a \textbf{coupled} arm, where $u$ steers the state, and a \textbf{decoupled}
arm, identical except that $u$ enters the cost and not the dynamics, so the
optimal gain is $0$ and the cancellation applies exactly. An effect present on
the coupled arm and absent on the decoupled one is attributed to the learning
channel rather than to representation, optimisation or capacity, which the
arms share. Every reference quantity the experiments compare against is
derived in \S\ref{sec:theory} and generated, not typed.

\subsection{Harness and measurement}

All experiments use one single-file PPO harness \citep{schulman2017ppo}
(GAE($\lambda$), clip 0.2, 4 epochs, 16 environments, 128-step rollouts,
15M steps; discount equal to the model's $\beta$), differing only in the policy
head: \textbf{mlp}, \textbf{gru128}, and two restricted heads that exist for
identification --- \textbf{linear} ($u=-kv$, which cannot leave the gain
manifold) and \textbf{linearb} ($u=-kv+b$, adding exactly the one escape
direction the theory computes). Both restricted heads can be initialised at a
chosen gain, and linearb can kick $b$ to $b_0$ at $t=0$, turning stability
claims into perturbation-decay measurements. Adding them left the mlp and
gru128 paths bitwise identical (Appendix~\ref{app:testbed}).

Deployed cost is measured with the policy mean on a single $200{,}000$-step
trajectory after burn-in. The obvious alternative, averaging short episodes,
would have manufactured a registered result: each reset pays the settling
transient, and the inflation grows with the gain (factors $1.088$, $1.270$,
$2.415$ at $k=1.65$, $5.99$, $20.55$), i.e.\ it is largest exactly where the
predicted effect is. Every continuous run also reports its \textbf{fitted
gain} (OLS slope of $u$ on $v$) and an \textbf{intermittency fraction} (share
of late-training evaluations costing more than $2J_{\mathrm{ml}}$), which
detects recurrent excursions without reference to any prediction. External
benchmarks are POPGym environments reimplemented natively with the upstream
seeding path reproduced and verified bitwise against the reference
implementation before any run.

\subsection{Audit protocol}

Claims are adjudicated by \emph{executable} preregistration: frozen constants
and scoring functions in a Python file whose freeze is machine-checked at
deployment and which refuses to certify a freeze if data for that experiment
already exist. Amendments are dated and disclosed in the file that carries
them; post-data amendments are labelled as such. Scorers for experiments whose
result files accumulate across submissions refuse duplicated cells; capability
gates are scored before predictions and a failed gate leaves its predictions
\emph{not scored}. Node class is recorded for every cluster job because CPU
generations are not bitwise comparable; the largest cross-class perturbation
we measured is $1.9$ seed standard deviations and moves no verdict. Two
departures from this discipline were found by a pre-submission audit and are
disclosed in \S\ref{sec:discussion}. Full protocol, harness settings and the
number-provenance table are in Appendix~\ref{app:testbed}.
